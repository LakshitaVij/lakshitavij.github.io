Hello, my name is Lakshita Vij! Chasing my curiosity has led me to a very non-linear path , and while its been a steep climb , Im enjoying the view :) So welcome to the ride!

I am currently a graduate student at NYU studying Computer Science, and I did my undergrad at UCLA majoring in Economics and Cognitive Science and minoring in CS and Film TV. I am passionate about bringing affordable and accessible healthcare for all at scale!

This past summer, I worked at NYU Langone and ChartR. At Langone, I developed multimodal deep learning for prostate cancer detection so we can prevent the number of unnecessary biopsies that happen. At ChartR, I built pipelines to evaluate clinical decision-support agents against real reports. Previously, I co-built Neuroscan360, a multimodal early detection system for neurodegenerative diseases with a 23 game suite across memory, motor coordination, speech, and vision.

I am actively looking for my next stint, so if you want someone to do pretty much anything for you, I can join!

I currently live in New York City, but I am happy to relocate. Home for me growing up has been split across India, China, Mauritius and now, the US, so change has been the only constant for me, so please feel free to reach out for an opportunity anywhere!

When I am not working, I enjoy reading, writing, going on long aimless walks, stopping by my friends house for 15 minutes that turns into 5 hours, and recently, surfing!
