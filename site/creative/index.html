FTV 4: Introduction to Art and Technique of Filmmaking

Fall 2020

I created a storyboard for Vertigo. I also edited a scene from Before Sunrise to see the difference between adding sound and leaving a scene silent, and what mood each can induce. It was only a slight change, but it made a great difference in the scene.

Spring 2021

Fall 2021

A study of the business models underlying today’s rapidly evolving film, television and new media industries. Coursework included research assignments, a marketing midterm and a final capstone project that pairs student teams with high-level industry mentors.
