Can I Watch It At Night?

Enter the name of a movie or TV show and, based on your threshold for scary, find out whether you can or should watch it at night.

The idea

Before you press play, you usually want to know one thing: is this going to scare me too much to watch at night? I built Can I Watch It At Night? to answer that. You type in a movie or show, tell it how much scary you can handle, and it tells you whether you can, or should, watch it at night.

What I built

A full-stack movie recommendation app. It reads a title’s plot sentiment and genre, compares them with the scare categories you pick from more than 20, and flags whether the title suits night-time viewing. In my evaluation, it got night-time suitability right 95.6% of the time.

It is built with React, Node.js and Express, and draws on the TMDB, OpenAI and Wikipedia APIs.

How it works

I curated a custom database of 9,500+ titles and gave each one vector embeddings, so recommendations could be tailored. In my own evaluations, that raised the relevance of the top three suggestions by 86%.

The backend uses TF-IDF and cosine similarity with weighted heuristics, and responds in a median of under 1.5 seconds. I also prototyped semantic filtering with OpenAI embeddings.

What's next

Semantic filtering is still a prototype. I tested OpenAI embeddings to match titles by meaning instead of keywords, but the live ranking runs on TF-IDF. Next, I would move it into the main recommendations and re-measure the 95.6% accuracy and the top-three relevance gain.
